Overview & health

Three questions, in order: is capture still arriving, is the recorded window continuous, and did the projection land. Any verdict below OK means the warehouse is not yet trustworthy for that concern.

Capture freshness

UNKNOWN

Stale above 60s of useful-data silence or recorded-head age; broken above 300s. UNKNOWN means no freshness signal was exposed at all.

Coverage continuity

UNKNOWN

Stale on any incident, open window, or non-zero gap-state gauge; broken on a sequence-gap, checksum, quarantine, or permanent-loss incident.

Projection integrity

UNKNOWN

Stale above 300s of dataset or warehouse projection lag, on any uncommitted dataset, or on coverage with no dataset; broken above 1800s or when every dataset is uncommitted.
